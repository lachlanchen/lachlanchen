# Image provenance

Method: built-in image generation. Approved seated concept from the project-owned original LazyingArt panda logo; transparent and compact-face derivatives from that approved concept. No external art, model API key or CLI generation was used. ImageMagick only packages sizes/formats after generation.

## Transparent derivative

The horizontal banner uses the same panda with the company-only wordmark. Existing profile README banner links stay unchanged and consume the new banner asset.

Use case: background-extraction. Edit the supplied approved LazyingArt seated panda logo. Change ONLY the exterior white background to genuine transparent alpha. Preserve exactly the panda's seated pose, tilted head, black shapes, crescent smiling eyes, W-shaped mouth, peach cheeks, green bamboo and leaves, proportions, colors and placement. Keep the white face, white belly, white paw pads and internal white outlines solid opaque white; they are part of the character, not background. Clean antialiased cutout edges, no white halo, no shadow, no checkerboard painted into pixels. Entire original panda and bamboo fully visible, same square canvas. No redesign, no simplification, no new details, no text. High-quality transparent PNG for a brand logo.

## Compact favicon derivative

Use case: logo-brand, favicon derivative. Input is the approved LazyingArt seated panda logo. Create its SMALL-SIZE ICON variant, not a new mascot. Use a close crop of exactly this panda's head: same softly tilted white round face, black rounded ears, black oval eye patches, small white crescent smiling eyes, tiny oval black nose, original W-shaped smile and peach cheeks. Keep these recognizable proportions and expression, with simplified clean crisp edges that read well at 16–48 pixels. Omit the body, feet and bamboo only for this compact head icon. Center the entire head and both ears with 12 percent safe margin inside a square canvas. Pure opaque white background, bold clean black shapes, peach cheeks. No text, no additional elements, no shadows, no 3D, no new expression. High-quality raster, one icon only.
