## Discovery policy

- Use HTTPS and the final public URL. GitHub project sites use `https://lachlan.lazying.art/<project>/`, not their redirecting github.io aliases.
- Index useful landing pages, public readers, free practice pages, and visitor-safe demonstrations. Keep prototypes distinct from available products.
- Keep signed-in content, login/reset pages, personal records, private repositories, APIs, and operational configuration out of the public directory and sitemaps. Authentication is the privacy boundary; robots rules are not access control.
- Retired and unverified destinations are excluded. Removing a directory entry does not delete its DNS or source repository.
- Search Console domain properties cover subdomains and project paths. Sitemaps help discovery; they do not guarantee indexing or ranking. Review actual queries and useful visitor actions, not just the number of indexed pages.
- Use each site's canonical URL; do not submit Medium copies and original blog posts as competing originals.

References: [Google: domain properties](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/34592), [sitemaps](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/sitemaps/build-sitemap), [noindex](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/block-indexing).

The maintained public data is [public-sites.json](public-sites.json). Additional public code, including projects without websites, is in [repositories.md](repositories.md). Private operational details are deliberately absent.
